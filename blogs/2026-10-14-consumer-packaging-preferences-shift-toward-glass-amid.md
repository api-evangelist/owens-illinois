Packaging is no longer an afterthought for consumers. New survey data shows consumer packaging preferences are increasingly shaped by concerns about chemicals in food packaging, microplastics and packaging materials. As a result, glass packaging is becoming a more important consideration in food and beverage purchase decisions.
